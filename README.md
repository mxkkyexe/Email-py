# Email-py 📋

One click. Every recipient address. Ready to paste.

Copies **To, Cc, and Bcc** (when Gmail shows it), skips duplicates, and leaves out the sender.

## Get it

1. Grab **Email-py.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/mxkkyexe/Email-py/releases/latest).
2. Unzip it.
3. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder.
5. Refresh Gmail. You're ready! ✨

## Use it

Open a message and click the copy icon beside the recipient details arrow. A check mark means the addresses are on your clipboard.

## Update

Get the latest release, replace the files in your extension folder, then reload the extension and Gmail.

Only runs on `mail.google.com`. Your message data stays in your browser.
